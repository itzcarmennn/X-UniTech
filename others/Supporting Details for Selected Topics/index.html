  • We selected the EV3 Medium Servo Motor because our robot weighs approximately 800 g and does not require the high torque provided by the EV3 Large Servo Motor.
  • With a maximum speed of 165 RPM, the Medium Motor offers higher rotational speed compared to the Large Motor (105 RPM). In our actual operation, we typically use only 65% of the motor speed, which is sufficient to move our robot effectively.
  • Therefore, the Medium Motor provides an ideal balance of speed, torque, and compactness without requiring the additional torque of a larger motor.

Engineering Decision：

  • The EV3 Medium Motor was selected instead of the EV3 Large Motor based on its higher reliability during the Open Challenge testing at 100% speed.
  • The Medium Motor achieved a 100% success rate (6/6 trials) with an average completion time of 25.67 seconds, while the Large Motor achieved only a 20% success rate (2/10 trials) despite being faster, with an average completion time of 18 seconds.
  • Although the Large Motor offered better speed, the Medium Motor provided more consistent performance.
  • Therefore, reliability and successful navigation were prioritised over maximum speed.

1.2.2 Gear Combination Selection

To further minimize the drivetrain size, we designed a compact gearbox using:

1 piece Z20 Double Bevel Gear (20 Teeth) – Part 18575

1 piece Differential Gear (28 Teeth) – Part 62821

3 piece Conical Wheels (12 Teeth) – Part 6589

Gear Ratio Calculation

DrivenGearTeeth = 28T

DrivingGearTeeth = 20T

Motor Speed = 165RPM

Torque = 6.64 N.CM

 

 

 6.64  1.4

  9.296 N·cm 

  • We selected a 1.4:1 gear reduction to balance motor speed and output torque. The 20T Z20 Double Bevel Gear transfers power from the EV3 Medium Motor to the 28T Differential Gear.
  • This reduction decreases rotational speed while increasing the theoretical output torque.
  • The differential gearbox, which contains three internal 12T bevel gears, distributes power to the left and right drive wheels while allowing them to rotate at different speeds during turns.
  • This helps the robot navigate corners smoothly while maintaining stable movement.
